# Pig
- Everybody loves a good gambling game. Pig allows you to bring the risky side of you out through wagering all of your turn's points to try to gather more.

- Get too greedy? Lose all of your progress. 
- Too shy to roll? You'll never beat Clover and our other AI opponents! 

---

## How To Play
- Visit our [Official Site](https://pig-u3vt.onrender.com/).

- Each player will have a turn and it will move to the next after one player's turn is up.

- A **Turn**: To begin a turn, you must roll the die. The amount you recieve is added to your total score this turn. After the first roll, you have the choice to either roll again and gain more points, or hold and have your current turn total compounded to your total game points.

- There is a catch! If you roll and recieve a **1**, you will lose all of the points you gained this turn! You will keep your total game points, but all of the progress you made this turn will be all for naught!

- The goal of the game is to reach the designated total points that you select in the start menu before others beat you to it.

- Choose a variety of different difficulties and game modes, like party play or compete against Clover or our other pigs. Make some bets with your friends, figure out your fantasy football draft order -> use Pig as your new quick "Coin Flip".
